Does Ratevo post reviews automatically? → No. Ratevo never posts automatically. The customer clicks 'Copy & Open Google Review', which copies the text to clipboard and opens Google Maps in a new tab. They paste and submit manually.

--- HOW IT WORKS ---
How Ratevo works
From QR code to Google review — powered by real customer feedback.

1. Create your business profile — Sign up and add your business details — name, category, address, and your Google Review link. Ratevo generates a unique slug for your business.
2. Get your QR code — Download a high-resolution QR code from your dashboard. Print it on table tents, receipts, window stickers, or menus — anywhere customers can scan.
3. Customers scan & rate — Customers scan the QR code with their phone camera. They select a 1-5 star rating, choose what stood out (optional), and add a sentence in their own words.
4. AI generates a review draft — Ratevo's AI creates a natural-sounding Google review draft using ONLY the customer's feedback. No invented details, no exaggeration — just their real experience in their voice.
5. Customer reviews & edits — The customer reads the draft, can edit it freely, or regenerate for a different tone. They stay in control of what gets posted.
6. One click to post on Google — Customer clicks 'Copy & Open Google Review' — the review text is copied to clipboard and Google Maps opens in a new tab. They paste and submit. Done.
